# Prove repo-level value before a central portal, self-service actions or federation

**All effort goes to repo portals until repo-level value is proven.** A repo portal is `toolbox report` with its ratchet, the `actions/report` action, the starter and packages. Five hypotheses must hold on at least three installed pilots, one of them maintained by people other than us: adoption takes one pull request; findings are true and worth fixing; coding agents improve repositories more with Toolbox than with the same standards written down; the ratchet catches real regressions without becoming a chore; and standards travel as packages without edits. Each has an experiment and a stop condition.

**Paused:** the central portal's deployment and operator loop (M6), self-service actions (M5, also paused by D64), federation (D68), and apps built on a portal's API. `serve`, REST, MCP, the UI, OpenFGA and Postgres (M2–M4) stay green in CI and get bug fixes, but no new features. Federation resumes from the packages that proved they travel.
