# D78. Example and experiment write-ups are catalogued by whether a reader can follow and check them

**Example and experiment write-ups are catalogued by whether a reader can follow and check them.** Toolbox for Toolbox gains an `Example` kind (READMEs under `examples/` and `experiments/`, plus `docs/examples`) and `maintainer-example`: bronze is a heading and resolving links; silver is a command that checks the example; gold is a pointer at the design or a decision, and no secret shape. The reference portal's `expected/` outputs are not regenerated. A new write-up must reach bronze
