# D80. Assessments and rubrics are scored too, and assessments are reviewed once

**Assessments and rubrics are scored too, and assessments are reviewed once.** Assessment records carry `unsuggested`, `uncited` and a content `hash`; `describe portal` emits `rubric` records (criteria, unguided criteria, assessments made, criteria every assessment passed). Seikika for Seikika gains a `Rubric` kind and the deterministic, ratcheted `meta-assessment` (suggestions, named assessor, cited findings, then cited everything) and `meta-rubric` (described, used, guided, discriminating); an `assessment-quality` rubric (supported, complete, reproducible, actionable) feeds the advisory `review-assessment`, which skips assessments made with `assessment-quality`, so reviews are never reviewed. Assessor independence and disagreement between assessors are proposed, not built
