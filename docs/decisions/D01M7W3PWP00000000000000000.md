# D72. Agent skills are catalogued and scored with deterministic checks; live evals are not a gate

**Agent skills are catalogued and scored with deterministic checks; live evals are not a gate.** Seikika for Seikika gains a `Skill` kind from `SKILL.md` (`workspace` `skills` globs) and `maintainer-skill`: bronze is the Agent Skills spec plus a third-person description that says when; silver is a short procedure with an example, resolving links and portable paths; gold is stated limits, at least three eval cases in `evals/evals.json`, no hidden Unicode, no hardcoded secret shapes, and at most one reference hop and three supporting files. Thresholds live in the manifest. SkillEvaluator's weighted score, SkillSpector's full scanner, and paired with/without-skill runs (SkillsBench, SkillEvaluator Tier 3, skill-creator, SkillPortrait) are the right next measurements and do not gate: they need a model or they are known to misfire on substrings. A new skill must reach bronze
