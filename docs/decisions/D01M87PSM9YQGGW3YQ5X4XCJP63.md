# Design review runs on stories, probes and two reference portals, and stops on a plateau

**The hosted report is built from ES modules bundled by esbuild into the one file the binary
embeds. Every page and shared component has Storybook stories over committed report fixtures from
two reference portals, Seikika's own and an OpenFGA repository's, plus synthetic edge cases. A story
probe checks every story in CI. A design-review round passes on probes, open severity and a panel
median, and the loop stops when the targeted scores stop moving.** This changes the stop rule of
the blind design audit (D01M85MK7FCRXH2TRMRKXTNA5GT) and how the report is built under the custom
element contract (D01M7X0RYD20000000000000000). The evidence is
[design-loop-efficiency-2026-11-24.md](../audits/design-loop-efficiency-2026-11-24.md).

1. **Modules, the same element.** The report's source lives in `report-ui/src/`, one module per
   view and per shared component. esbuild bundles it into
   `crates/seikika-cli/src/seikika-report.js`, which stays committed and embedded, so `seikika
   report -o web` needs no Node. CI rebuilds the bundle and fails if the committed copy differs.
   The `<seikika-report>` element, its attributes, slots and JSON input are unchanged.
2. **No second framework.** The report stays framework-free. If a view outgrows plain DOM code,
   it moves to React, the UI default (D31), not to another framework (D45).
3. **Stories over fixtures.** `report-ui/fixtures/` holds report JSON from Seikika for Seikika, the
   OpenFGA portal (`examples/openfga-portal`), and small synthetic portals for states the real
   ones lack. Each fixture is regenerated by a script, not edited by hand. Storybook 10
   (`@storybook/web-components-vite`) shows each page per fixture and each shared component in its
   states.
4. **Probes before auditors.** `report-ui` runs a probe over every story at 1280×800 and 390×844
   in CI. It fails on serious axe-core violations (WCAG 2.2 AA), horizontal overflow, or interactive
   targets under 24 px. Mechanical defects are caught here, in seconds, rather than by the next
   blind auditor.
5. **A noise-aware stop rule.** A round passes when the probes pass, no severity-3 or 4 finding is
   open, and the median of a panel of three blind auditors does not fall on any dimension the
   round targeted. The loop stops when two passing rounds in a row raise no targeted median, or
   when every targeted dimension is at 4. One auditor's score is not a verdict: in 22 plateau
   rounds, a dimension fell in 14% of comparisons and rose in 14%.
6. **Two reference portals.** Each round captures both reference portals, and its tasks include
   at least one on the OpenFGA portal. A finding that appears on one portal only is fixed in the box
   when it is the box's, and in the portal when it is the portal's. The record says which.

## Consequences

- The design-audit skill's capture takes a Storybook build or a served report, and its stop rule
  is the one above.
- The maintainer SDLC gains a `design-review` stage, scored by `sdlc-stage` like the others, and
  the SDLC stage rubric asks whether each stage gives fast feedback and is checked on more than
  one reference.
- A panel costs three auditors per round instead of one, but the loop should end in far fewer
  rounds than 36.
